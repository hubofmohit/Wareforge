2. How the access-link model affects your privacy

Because there's no login system, access to your data is controlled entirely by possession of your private link — not by verifying who a person is. Practically, this means:

  • Anyone who has your link can view and edit all of your data. There's no way for us to distinguish between you and someone else who obtained the link.
  • If your link ends up in a shared inbox, a forwarded email, browser history on a shared computer, or similar, whoever finds it has the same access you do.
  • We can't offer per-person access logs, audit trails tied to individual identities, or the ability to revoke one person's access while keeping another's — because the system has no concept of individual users, only the link itself.

Treat your link the way you'd treat a password.
